Sidebar colours

Both follow your system's light or dark setting.

New bookmarks go

Of the folder, or of the bookmarks bar: for a tab or group dropped on a folder or on the Bookmarks heading, and for Bookmark tab, Bookmark all tabs, Save group to bookmarks, Add current tab and New folder. Dropped between two bookmarks, it goes where you drop it.

Expand all opens

For the expand buttons on the Bookmarks heading and on folders with subfolders. Folders you mark “Don't expand with Expand all” (right-click a folder) are skipped either way; collapsing always closes everything below.

Site icons

Off by default.

When Chrome hasn't stored a bookmarked page's icon yet (you haven't visited it), the panel asks that page's site for its icon file, as a browser does on a visit; like a visit, it follows the site if it sends that request on to where it keeps its icon. No other service is used, and tabs never trigger it.

Nightly sweep

Off by default. The selected tab of the focused window, tabs playing audio, pinned tabs and tabs in a group stay open, windows are never closed, and bookmarks are never changed. The first sweep after switching on is the next 03:00.

Backup

Your bookmark icons, folder colours, settings and open folders are kept by the extension, so uninstalling it deletes them (reloading it at chrome://extensions keeps them). Export them to a file first; Import puts them back, on the same bookmarks.
